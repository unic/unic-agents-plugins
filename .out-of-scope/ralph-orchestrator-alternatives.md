# Ralph Orchestrator Alternatives

This project will not research alternatives to the ralph-orchestrator.

## Why this is out of scope

`docs/plans/` and the ralph-orchestrator workflow built on top of it are being discontinued. Researching replacement orchestrators for a system that is being phased out is not a productive use of time. New work follows the `docs/issues/` triage workflow instead.
